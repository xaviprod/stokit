What it does

About Bulx, in detail.

The origin

A production company with a gear warehouse, a vehicle fleet and a team that goes out on shoots needed to know where everything was and who had it. The inventory came out of that, and the rest followed.

How it was built

Each part was requested by the warehouse, production or the technical department, tested on a real shoot and corrected with what was learned.

The name and the logo

The six areas (gear, fleet, quotes, IT, crew and finance) work like connected cells: a quote reserves gear, a booking takes up a vehicle. The logo is by Xavi Murillo.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

If your question is not here, write to info@bulx.es.

Who is behind Bulx?

Palma Pictures, a production company in Mallorca, and Xavi Murillo, who is responsible for the product and designed the logo. Contact: info@bulx.es.

Why is it called Bulx?

After the honeycomb: separate, connected cells, like the areas of a production company.

Do other production companies use it?

It is beginning to be offered outside Palma Pictures. The first production companies interested can request a demo.

A demo with your own gear.

Thirty minutes by video call, with your gear or vehicle spreadsheet. No commitment and nothing to install.

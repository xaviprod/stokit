What it does

Finance, in detail.

Quote status

Drafts, sent, accepted and invoiced, with their amounts and their age. What has moved this month and what has gone a long time without a reply.

By project and by client

Amount for each project and each client over the year, average amount per quote and time to acceptance.

Excel report

An Excel workbook with the tabs and charts already built, for a management meeting or for the accountant.

Power BI

Bulx publishes its tables as an OData data source. Existing Power BI dashboards read the data directly, with no export step.

In-house cost

What a project costs in staff hours against the amount invoiced, by job title, department and project, with configurable rates.

Restricted access

Amounts are visible only to people with Finance permission. The rest of the team works without access to prices or totals.

Getting started

Three steps to get started.

  1. 1

    Quote in Bulx

    The financial data is derived from the quotes; it is not entered separately.

  2. 2

    Mark what has been invoiced

    When the invoice is issued in the accounting software, the quote is marked as invoiced.

  3. 3

    Review

    In Bulx, in the Excel report or in Power BI.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

If your question is not here, write to info@bulx.es.

Does it integrate with accounting software?

Not directly. Bulx does not issue invoices; quotes are marked as invoiced and the data is exported to Excel or read from Power BI.

What data is available to Power BI?

The same tables Bulx uses: quotes, projects, clients, gear and fleet, through a read-only user.

Are previous years kept?

Yes. Data is kept by year and by month from the first quote recorded.

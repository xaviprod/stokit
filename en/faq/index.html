FAQ

Every question, by area.

If your question is not here, write to info@bulx.es.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Bulx is a web application used from the browser. The iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch app is optional and is meant for work done on your feet: scanning, booking and clocking in.

Can the current inventory be imported?

Yes. It is imported from an Excel sheet; Bulx organises it by department and assigns a code to each item. Anything that does not fit is flagged for review.

Who sees what?

Each person sees the areas they have permission for: production, fleet, gear, IT. Prices and finance are only visible with Finance permission.

Where is the data stored?

On servers in Frankfurt (Germany), with backups. Every change is recorded with the person, the action and the date.

What size of production company is it meant for?

It was developed at a production company with over 700 items, 29 vehicles and 180 people between staff and crew. It also works for much smaller volumes; there is no minimum.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the volume of gear and the number of people. It is set out in writing at the demo.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The web version is adapted to mobile screens and there is also an app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch with a label scanner, bookings and chat.

Can it be tried before signing up?

Yes. There is a 14-day free trial that can be started from this website, and the demo uses the production company’s own gear.

Can it be used to rent gear out to third parties?

Yes. Each item has a price per day, quotes are built from the catalogue and the gear is reserved for the accepted dates.

Is a barcode scanner needed?

No. A phone camera is enough. The label opens a public page that shows neither serial numbers nor prices.

How many items can it hold?

There is no practical limit. The production company where it was developed manages more than 700, with filters by category, warehouse, project and status.

Does it handle vehicles hired for a project?

Yes. A vehicle can be owned or hired for specific dates, and it appears in the same calendar for that period.

Who can make bookings?

People with edit permission in Fleet. Everyone else can view the calendar without changing it. Permissions are assigned per person.

What happens with two bookings on the same dates?

The calendar does not allow bookings for the same vehicle to overlap: the second person sees that the vehicle is taken, and by whom.

Does Bulx issue invoices?

No. Bulx handles the quote up to acceptance; the invoice is issued with your usual accounting software. The quote is then marked as invoiced so that the finance figures reconcile.

Does the client need an account?

No. The link opens without registration. It has an expiry date and can be revoked.

Can quotes be broken down by department?

Yes. Camera, lighting, grip or any sub-project, each with its subtotal, plus the project total.

Is any other software needed?

No. Bulx runs in the browser and in the iPhone and iPad app; it does not depend on Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace.

Does it manage licences from any vendor?

Yes. Each vendor has its record with the seats purchased, the people assigned and the renewal date.

Does it include landlines?

Yes. Mobile and fixed lines, with extension, provider and account holder.

Can people clock in from a phone?

Yes. From the iPhone and from the Apple Watch. The hours are stored on the person’s record and on the project record.

Does external crew need access?

It is not necessary. They are listed on the project record with their role and contact details. Limited access can be granted if required.

Does it replace the team’s messaging app?

For anything related to projects, yes: the chat includes references to the quote, the vehicle or the gear with their status, which avoids forwarding screenshots.

Does it integrate with accounting software?

Not directly. Bulx does not issue invoices; quotes are marked as invoiced and the data is exported to Excel or read from Power BI.

What data is available to Power BI?

The same tables Bulx uses: quotes, projects, clients, gear and fleet, through a read-only user.

Are previous years kept?

Yes. Data is kept by year and by month from the first quote recorded.

Is there an Android version?

Not at present. The app is for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. On Android, the web version is used from the browser; it is adapted to mobile screens.

Can quotes be prepared in the app?

No. They can be viewed and their status followed. Preparing and sending them is done on the web.

Does it work without signal?

Viewing data that is already loaded and scanning both work. Changes are sent when the connection returns.

Where is the data stored?

On servers in Frankfurt (Germany), in the European Union, with backups. Documents and photos are stored in the same region.

Can all the data be exported?

Yes. Gear, fleet, people, quotes and finance are exported to Excel; documents are exported as they are.

Who at Bulx has access to the data?

Only the staff who maintain the service, when the production company requests support, and that access is logged like any other.

Does anything need to be installed for the demo?

No. It is a video call and a web page. Afterwards, access to a trial environment can be provided on request.

How much does Bulx cost?

It depends on the volume of gear and the number of people. It is set out in writing at the demo.

How long does it take to get started?

With the Excel sheet, one afternoon to import and label. Once the accounts have been created, the team can sign in the next day.

Who is behind Bulx?

Palma Pictures, a production company in Mallorca, and Xavi Murillo, who is responsible for the product and designed the logo. Contact: info@bulx.es.

Why is it called Bulx?

After the honeycomb: separate, connected cells, like the areas of a production company.

Do other production companies use it?

It is beginning to be offered outside Palma Pictures. The first production companies interested can request a demo.

A demo with your own gear.

Thirty minutes by video call, with your gear or vehicle spreadsheet. No commitment and nothing to install.

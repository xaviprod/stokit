What it does

Crew and shoots, in detail.

Staff and crew

Job title, department, contact details and the gear, devices and lines assigned. The crew of each project, with their roles and contact details.

Hours and in-house days

Each person logs their working day and their shooting days. Management sees the cost of each project in staff hours and compares it with the amount invoiced.

Internal chat

Conversations by project or by person. A message can include the card of a quote, a vehicle or an item, which shows its up-to-date status.

Permissions by area

Production, fleet, gear, IT, finance: per person and per level (read, edit, full). Amounts are visible only to those with Finance permission.

Projects and calendar

Each project with its dates, team, gear and quotes, and a production calendar showing each week’s shoots.

Announcements

Notices shown at sign-in: maintenance, a new feature, a presentation. Scheduled or immediate; only administrators can publish them.

Getting started

Three steps to get started.

  1. 1

    Add the people

    From an Excel sheet or one by one, each with a username and password.

  2. 2

    Assign permissions

    With templates by role (production, fleet, technician, management) and individual adjustments.

  3. 3

    Log hours

    From the app or the Apple Watch. They are allocated to the current project.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

If your question is not here, write to info@bulx.es.

Can people clock in from a phone?

Yes. From the iPhone and from the Apple Watch. The hours are stored on the person’s record and on the project record.

Does external crew need access?

It is not necessary. They are listed on the project record with their role and contact details. Limited access can be granted if required.

Does it replace the team’s messaging app?

For anything related to projects, yes: the chat includes references to the quote, the vehicle or the gear with their status, which avoids forwarding screenshots.

What it does

Production company software, in detail.

Gear inventory

One record per item, with serial number, photos, category and status. Kits with their contents, dimensions and weight per case, and a record of every check-out and every return.

Production fleet

Vehicle booking calendar by project, MOT (roadworthiness test) and insurance reminders, breakdown reports from the phone and check-out and return photos.

Rental quotes

Built from the catalogue, with a price per day. The client accepts them online from a link and the gear is reserved for those dates.

Staff and crew

Staff and crew records, hours allocated to the project from the phone or the Apple Watch, permissions by area and internal chat.

Finance

Quoted, accepted and invoiced amounts by project, client and month, with an Excel report and a connection for Power BI.

IT inventory

Laptops, phones, lines, MiFi routers and licences assigned to each person, with the return list when someone leaves.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

If your question is not here, write to info@bulx.es.

What is management software for audiovisual production companies?

It is software that runs the operational side of a production company: what gear there is and where it is, which vehicles are booked, what has been quoted and accepted, and who is working on each project. It is not video editing software or shoot scheduling software.

How does it differ from a project management tool or a generic ERP?

It starts from the way a production company works: items that go out on a shoot and come back, kits, vehicles with MOT dates, quotes by day and by department, crew by project. Generic software requires that work to be adapted to its screens.

What kind of production company is it for?

For advertising, film, television and production service companies that have their own gear, vehicles or a team that goes out on shoots, and for equipment rental houses.

Does it replace accounting software?

No. Bulx handles the quote up to acceptance; the invoice is issued with the usual accounting software and the quote is marked as invoiced.

Is there a trial?

Yes. There is a 14-day free trial, and a demo by video call using the production company’s own gear.

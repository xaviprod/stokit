What it does

App, in detail.

Label scanner

Gear, vehicles and devices. The camera reads the label and opens the record; the check-out or return is recorded on the spot.

Vehicle bookings

Choose the vehicle type and the days. The app shows availability and who has booked the other vehicles.

Chat and notifications

Team conversations, with quote, vehicle and gear cards, and notifications when there are messages or changes that affect the person.

Apple Watch

Clocking in and out and checking the day’s hours. The watch syncs with the iPhone.

Widgets

Free vehicles, gear out and the day’s hours on the home screen, without opening the app.

iPad

The same app, adapted to the iPad screen for fleet, gear and project records.

Getting started

Three steps to get started.

  1. 1

    Install the app

    From the App Store, for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

  2. 2

    Sign in

    With Apple, with Google or with the Bulx username; after that, with Face ID.

  3. 3

    Scan

    Anything recorded in the app appears on the web for the whole team.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

If your question is not here, write to info@bulx.es.

Is there an Android version?

Not at present. The app is for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. On Android, the web version is used from the browser; it is adapted to mobile screens.

Can quotes be prepared in the app?

No. They can be viewed and their status followed. Preparing and sending them is done on the web.

Does it work without signal?

Viewing data that is already loaded and scanning both work. Changes are sent when the connection returns.

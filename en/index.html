The product

Six areas, connected to each other.

Each area works on its own and shares its data with the others: an accepted quote reserves the gear, a booking blocks a vehicle in the calendar and each person sees the project they are assigned to.

Gear and inventory

Gear records with serial number, photo and QR label. Kits with their contents, dimensions and weight per case, and the status of each item: in the warehouse, on a project or in repair.

How it works

Three steps to get started.

  1. 1

    Import the current data

    The gear spreadsheet, the vehicle list and the people list. Bulx organises them by department and assigns a code to each item.

  2. 2

    Print and attach the labels

    One label per case, item or vehicle, printed from Bulx. Scanning it with a phone records who takes it, when and for which project. Nothing to install.

  3. 3

    Quote and reserve

    Quotes are built from the catalogue. When the client accepts, the gear and the vehicles are reserved for those dates and cannot be assigned to another project.

A shooting day

A shooting day, step by step.

  1. 07:10

    Vehicle check-out

    Marta scans the label on V-02. It records who is taking it, at what mileage and for which project.

  2. 08:30

    Gear check-out

    The lighting kit cases leave the warehouse. They are the ones listed in the accepted quote, and the total weight for transport is already calculated.

  3. 12:15

    A breakdown

    A blown tail light. The report is sent from the phone with a photo and creates a workshop order before the vehicle is back.

  4. 17:45

    A quote is accepted

    The client opens the quote link for the next project and accepts it. The gear is reserved for those dates.

  5. 20:30

    Return

    The gear is scanned on return. If anything is missing or damaged, it is flagged in the return and on the item record.

Security

Access, permissions and audit log.

Production data belongs to the production company. Bulx documents who accesses it, what they see and what they change.

Sign in with Apple or GoogleEach person can link their Apple or Google account and sign in with one click, without typing a password.
Two-step verificationRequired for everyone: a six-digit code from an authenticator app when signing in and every seven days, no SMS.
Permissions by areaProduction, fleet, gear, IT and finance, with read, edit or full levels.
Activity logEvery change is recorded with the person, the action and the date. The log cannot be deleted.
Servers in EuropeData is stored in Frankfurt, with backups.
Expiring linksAnything shared externally, such as a quote or a calendar, expires and can be revoked.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

If your question is not here, write to info@bulx.es.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Bulx is a web application used from the browser. The iPhone and iPad app is optional, meant for scanning, booking and clocking in.

Can the current inventory be imported?

Yes. It is imported from an Excel sheet; Bulx organises it by department and assigns a code to each item. Anything that does not fit is flagged for review.

Who sees what?

Each person sees the areas they have permission for: production, fleet, gear or IT. Prices and finance are only visible with Finance permission.

Where is the data stored?

On servers in Frankfurt (Germany), with backups. Every change is recorded with the person, the action and the date.

What size of production company is it meant for?

It was developed at a production company with over 700 items, 29 vehicles and 180 people between staff and crew. There is no minimum volume.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the volume of gear and the number of people. It is set out in writing at the demo.

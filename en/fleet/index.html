Fleet

The production fleet, in a booking calendar.

Each vehicle has its record, its documents with their expiry dates and its history. Bookings are made by project and by day; check-outs and returns are recorded by scanning the vehicle label.

What it does

Fleet, in detail.

Booking calendar

Week or month view, one vehicle per row. Bookings are linked to a project and cannot overlap. Workshop and MOT periods take up their slot in the same calendar.

MOT, insurance and servicing

Each vehicle stores its MOT, insurance and service dates. Upcoming expiries appear on the home page and under Tasks / MOT; appointments are noted and the result is recorded.

Label on the vehicle

Scanning the label records who takes the vehicle, at what mileage and for which project. On return, the mileage and the condition of the vehicle are recorded.

Inspections with photos

Check-out and return photos at every use. New damage can be attributed to the project and the date on which it appeared.

Breakdown reports

Breakdowns are reported from the phone with photos and a description. They create a workshop order and stay in the vehicle history with their cost.

Checklists

What has to be checked before each check-out and the pending tasks for each vehicle, with a person responsible and a date.

Getting started

Three steps to get started.

  1. 1

    Add the vehicles

    Registration number, type, mileage, MOT and insurance. They can also be imported from Excel.

  2. 2

    Attach the label

    One per vehicle, printed from Bulx.

  3. 3

    Book by project

    From the web or from the app. Check-outs and returns are recorded with the label.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

If your question is not here, write to info@bulx.es.

Does it handle vehicles hired for a project?

Yes. A vehicle can be owned or hired for specific dates, and it appears in the same calendar for that period.

Who can make bookings?

People with edit permission in Fleet. Everyone else can view the calendar without changing it. Permissions are assigned per person.

What happens with two bookings on the same dates?

The calendar does not allow bookings for the same vehicle to overlap: the second person sees that the vehicle is taken, and by whom.

What it does

IT, in detail.

Devices

Laptops, phones, tablets and monitors with serial number, warranty, assigned person and assignment date. Each has its own QR label.

Staff leavers

When a person leaves, Bulx produces the list of what has to be returned and closed: devices, lines, MiFi routers, keys and access rights, with a deadline and follow-up.

Lines and MiFi routers

Each line with its provider, account holder, PIN and PUK. MiFi routers are assigned to a project when they go out and are then returned, like the gear.

Licences

By vendor: seats purchased, the people using them and the renewal date. Upcoming renewals appear on the Technology home page.

Company emails

The production company’s email library and each person’s signature, ready to use from their email client.

Renewals in view

Warranties and licences that are about to expire appear on the Technology home page before they lapse.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

If your question is not here, write to info@bulx.es.

Is any other software needed?

No. Bulx runs in the browser and in the iPhone and iPad app; it does not depend on Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace.

Does it manage licences from any vendor?

Yes. Each vendor has its record with the seats purchased, the people assigned and the renewal date.

Does it include landlines?

Yes. Mobile and fixed lines, with extension, provider and account holder.
